# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Endpoint a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and
responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention,
personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information, or conduct inappropriate in a professional
setting. Investigation discussions must avoid unsupported accusations and real-world identity claims.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will clarify and enforce these standards and may remove contributions or ban
participants for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Report conduct issues
privately to the maintainers through the repository's security/contact channels.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
